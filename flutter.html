Debug Flutter apps

Breakpoints (conditional, and logpoints), stepping, variables, a console, and hot reload on save.

Try it

  1. Click the margin to set a breakpoint, then press Debug.
  2. When it stops: the call stack, variables, and a console that evaluates Dart.
  3. Step, continue, and hot reload; saving a Dart file hot reloads it.

Widget rebuild counts

See how often each of your widgets rebuilds, beside its line in the editor and in a list of the hottest widgets.

Try it

  1. Debug the app, then press Count Rebuilds in the Debug panel.
  2. The editor shows each widget's count after its line, colored from quiet to hot.
  3. The list puts the hottest first and flags widgets rebuilt in every frame; click one to open it.
