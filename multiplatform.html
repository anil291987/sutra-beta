Targets and shared previews

Preview shared composables with their composeResources strings and images, and run the Android, iOS, desktop or web target.

Try it

  1. Open a shared composable in commonMain and press Preview: strings and images come from composeResources.
  2. Pick the target in the run bar: Android, iOS Simulator, desktop (JVM) or web.
  3. Run builds and starts it.

The videos show Sutra with sample projects. Builds, devices and AI replies in them are simulated, so they always look the same; on your Mac they're real.
